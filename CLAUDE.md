# Claude working agreement

**At the start of any new chat, read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before making changes.**
It is the contributor guide and the source of truth for this project's
constraints (local-first, no telemetry/auth/cloud, typed command boundary,
scaled-integer weights, transactional landings, backup-integrity rules, and the
release/signing HANDOFF protocol).

Also follow the documentation rule in AGENTS.md: record proposed, planned, and
in-progress capabilities in [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md), and once a capability is
implemented and verified, move it to the `Implemented workflows` section of
[README.md](README.md).
